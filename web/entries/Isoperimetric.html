The Isoperimetric Inequality for Rectifiable Simple Closed Curves

Lawrence C. Paulson 📧

October 29, 2026

Abstract

This entry formalises the planar isoperimetric inequality for rectifiable simple closed curves. If a curve has length $L$, the area of its inside is at most $L^2/(4\pi)$; equality implies that its image is a circle. The development proves a Wirtinger inequality, a special area formula for convex curves, and a convexification argument. It builds on the Fourier AFP entry and analysis material incorporated into Isabelle2026.
