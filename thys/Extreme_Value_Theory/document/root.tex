\title{Extreme Value Theory}
\author{Michikazu Hirata}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
  This entry is a formalization of extreme value theory (EVT).
  EVT is a branch of probability theory and statistics that studies \emph{rare events}.
  We formalize two main results of EVT, mainly following the books
  by de Haan and Ferreira~\cite{deHaanFerreira}
  and by Embrechts et al.~\cite{EmbrechtsKluppelbergMikosch}.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item The Fisher--Tippett--Gnedenko theorem, stating that
  if the properly normalized maximum of i.i.d.~random variables converges
  in distribution to a non-degenerate limit, then the limit is an extreme value
  distribution.
  \item The Pickands--Balkema--de Haan theorem, stating that
  the conditional distribution of the excesses over a threshold $u$
  converges uniformly in the excess, with a suitable scale $\beta(u)$,
  to a generalized Pareto distribution
  as $u$ tends to the right endpoint of the support,
  if and only if the underlying distribution belongs
  to the maximum domain of attraction of an extreme value distribution.
  \end{itemize}
\end{abstract}
