\title{Finite-Dimensional Algebras and Scalar Towers}
\author{Arthur Freitas Ramos \and David Barros Hulak \and Ruy J. G. B. de Queiroz}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
For a finite-dimensional vector space over a finite-dimensional field extension, the
dimension over the smaller field is the product of the two dimensions.
The result includes vector subspaces and the zero space.  For compatible towers of two fields and a finite-dimensional commutative
unital algebra, trace and norm over the bottom field are the iterated trace
and norm through the intermediate field.  Examples
include $\mathbb F_2\subseteq\mathbb F_4$ and
$\mathbb R\subseteq\mathbb C$.  The development follows the standard finite
extension treatment in \cite{Lang2002}.
\end{abstract}
